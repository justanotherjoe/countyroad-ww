About Us

Wood, Craft, & Passion

County Road WW was built on a simple belief: that the best furniture is made by hand, with care, using the finest materials. We're a small workshop that takes on select projects so every piece gets the attention it deserves.

Whether you're looking for a custom kitchen, a statement dining table, or a one-of-a-kind piece for your home, we'll work with you from first sketch to final finish.
