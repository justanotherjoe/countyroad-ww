About Us

Wood, Craft, & Passion

County Road Woodworking was built on a simple belief: that the best work is made by hand, with care, using the finest materials. We're a family-owned shop, and every project — big or small — gets treated like it's going into our own home.

Whether you're looking for a custom kitchen, a commercial build-out, a statement dining table, or doors and millwork for any space, we'll work with you from first sketch to final finish. Everything we build is made in-house — ensuring that quality is done at every step.
